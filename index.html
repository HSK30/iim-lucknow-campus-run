Campus Run

Run laps around the campus road loop. You steer, the road does not.

  • Steering: tap a button for a small turn, hold it for a big one. On a curve you only turn when you press
  • Off the road: grass slows you down, the fence ends the run
  • Game over if you touch: a tree, a pothole (or jump it!), a PComm fight, or a prof who catches you cheating or sleeping
  • Party: you stumble around for 2 to 3 seconds. Case study: you jump on your own for 4 to 5 seconds
  • Speed breaker: -10 points. Snacks: +10 to +70, streaks multiply. Lap: +100
Left / Right (or A / D): steer  |  Up / Space: jump
Touch: hold left or right side to steer, tap the middle to jump
